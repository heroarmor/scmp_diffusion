Read: the adaptive advantage scales with budget. At E=0.75 (L96) adaptive beats uniform on both axes (KID 3.12 vs 3.41, PSNR 25.0 vs 23.1) — a Pareto win. But at E=0.375 (L48) adaptive is only tied on FID/PSNR and worse on KID (4.21 vs 2.95): uniform-48 is the KID sweet spot, and at a tiny budget there's little to reallocate. Uniform collapses at E=0.25 (L32) (KID 8.48, off-scale).
